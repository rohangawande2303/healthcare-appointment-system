PHASE 3 DOCUMENTATION - Healthcare Appointment System
===================================================

1. RAZORPAY INTEGRATION
-----------------------
How the payment system works:
1. When you click "Pay & Confirm", the app talks to our backend to create a "Payment Order" with Razorpay.
2. A secure Razorpay window pops up where you can pay using Cards, UPI, or NetBanking (we use Test Mode, so no real money is involved).
3. Once the payment is successful, Razorpay gives a "Secret Signature".
4. Our backend verifies this signature to make sure the payment was genuine and not faked.
5. Once verified, your appointment status is updated to 'Confirmed' automatically.

2. PAYMENT SECURITY
-------------------
- We never store your card details on our server.
- All payments are handled directly by Razorpay's secure servers.
- We use HMAC SHA256 encryption to verify every transaction, ensuring that no one can trick the system into thinking they paid without actually doing so.

3. WEBRTC VIDEO SYSTEM
----------------------
How video calling works (in simple terms):
- WebRTC is a technology that allows two browsers to talk directly to each other (Peer-to-Peer).
- Imagine two people wanting to talk. They first need to find each other's "address".
- Our backend acts as a "Signaling Server" using Socket.io to help the Doctor and Patient exchange their "digital addresses".
- Once they have each other's info, the video and audio travel directly between them for the fastest possible connection.

4. VIDEO CALL FLOW
------------------
1. Doctor clicks "Start Call" from their dashboard.
2. Patient sees a "Join Video Consultation" button on their dashboard.
3. Both join a private "Consultation Room".
4. They can see/hear each other, mute their mic, or turn off their camera.
5. When the call ends, both are safely returned to their dashboards.

5. DATABASE UPDATES
-------------------
- New 'payments' table stores transaction IDs and amounts.
- 'appointments' table now tracks 'payment_status' (pending/paid).

6. TESTING RESULTS
------------------
- Razorpay Test Mode: Successfully processed payments using test card numbers.
- Video Quality: Low latency communication verified on local network.
- Signaling: Socket.io successfully handles user join/leave events.

7. DEMO GUIDE FOR TEACHER
-------------------------
1. Login as Patient and go to book an appointment.
2. In the final step, click "Pay & Confirm".
3. Use Razorpay Test Card (4111 1111 1111 1111) to complete payment.
4. Go to 'My Appointments' and see the status as 'Confirmed'.
5. If it's a Video appointment, click "Join Video Consultation".
6. In another window, login as Doctor and click "Start Call" to begin the consultation.

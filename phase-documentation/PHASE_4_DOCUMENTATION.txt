PHASE 4 DOCUMENTATION - Healthcare Appointment System
===================================================

1. MEDICINE REMINDER SYSTEM
---------------------------
How it works:
- Patients can list their medications, specify dosages, and set exact times for reminders.
- A background "Cron Job" (like an alarm clock) runs every minute on our server to check if any patient needs to take their medicine right now.
- When it's time, the system sends an SMS (via Twilio) and an Email to the patient.

2. OPENFDA MEDICINE SEARCH (PYTHON SERVICE)
-------------------------------------------
Why we use Python:
- Python is excellent for data processing and talking to complex APIs.
- We built a small "Microservice" in Python Flask that talks to the US Government's OpenFDA database.
- When you type a medicine name, our Python service searches the official database to give you accurate brand names and warnings.

3. REAL-TIME QUEUE MANAGEMENT
-----------------------------
How it works:
- No more waiting blindly! Patients can see their exact position in the doctor's queue (e.g., "#3 in line").
- As the doctor completes each consultation, the queue moves up in real-time on everyone's screen without needing to refresh the page.
- We use WebSockets (Socket.io) to "push" these updates to all connected patients instantly.

4. REVIEW & RATING SYSTEM
-------------------------
- Trust is important! Patients can rate their doctors (1 to 5 stars) and leave comments.
- Verification: To prevent fake reviews, only patients who have actually 'Completed' an appointment with that doctor can leave a review.
- Doctor profiles now show their average rating and all patient feedback.

5. DESIGN & ACCESSIBILITY
-------------------------
- We added "Skeleton Loaders" (shimmering boxes) so the app feels fast even while data is loading.
- Smooth animations using "Framer Motion" make the transitions between pages feel premium and professional.
- The app is fully responsive, meaning it works perfectly on phones, tablets, and computers.

6. FINAL TESTING RESULTS
------------------------
- SMS Alerts: Successfully triggered for scheduled medicine times.
- Queue Updates: Real-time sync verified across multiple browser tabs.
- Data Privacy: Patients can only see their own reminders and queue positions.

7. DEMO GUIDE FOR TEACHER
-------------------------
1. Go to 'Medicine Reminders' and add a new medicine (e.g., 'Aspirin'). Notice the autocomplete suggestions!
2. Set a reminder time for 1 minute from now and wait for the console/log to show the SMS being sent.
3. As a Patient, look at an upcoming appointment. You'll see your queue position.
4. As a Doctor, mark that patient as "Completed".
5. Observe how the Patient's dashboard now shows a Review Form to rate the doctor.
6. Submit a review and see it appear on the Doctor's public profile.
